How to Get Started...

Getting into vinyl can feel like a lot as there is so much to consider. Follow these four steps and keep your budget in mind from the beginning.

1. Set a budget

Decide the total you can spend. Count the records as well, because the player is only the start.

2. Pick a turntable

Choose a reliable entry-level deck. One with a built-in preamp saves you buying extra gear.

3. Add speakers

Powered speakers have the amplifier built in, so there is one less item to buy.

4. Buying your first records

Buy your first records second-hand from shops and independent record stores to keep the cost down.

Essential Items - Find a suitable match for you and add up the price

  • The turntable
  • Speakers, or an amp and speakers
  • A preamp, if the turntable does not have one
  • A carbon fibre brush and a spare stylus
  • Your first five records
  • A little extra for upgrades later if you like
